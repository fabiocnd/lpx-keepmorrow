## 3. What the Current Development Application Does

The application reads supported regular files, directories, and ZIP exports that
you select. It preserves original files, builds a catalog under an experimental
source profile, retains conversation revisions and available branch structure,
records explicitly supported artifact relationships, and provides local browse,
search, and recovery operations. Original ZIP containers are retained alongside
their supported extracted contents. File bytes and inventory are verified against
a manifest. Source content and previous archive generations remain unchanged.

An explicit association decision creates a new local generation and records the
operator's reason without rewriting historical source evidence. Selected recovery
contains derived conversation records and available bound files. It excludes whole
recognized conversation export documents to avoid including unrelated conversations;
it does not inspect or redact the internal contents of arbitrary selected attachments.

The private service adds an authenticated portal, scoped acceptance records,
reviewed background operations, temporary upload intake, and verified portable
recovery packages. Local operator mode remains on loopback and has no MCP
endpoint. OAuth mode requires an explicitly configured identity provider and
permits an authorized MCP client to request accepted content. The account
connection and production deployment remain separate release gates; their
presence in the source does not establish that either has occurred.

The portal must display the selected resource, exact generation, permitted
actions, policies, and recipients before acceptance. An upload is a separately
authorized transfer into the configured operator's environment. Import,
association, recovery, packaging, and unpacking require an additional review of
the exact prepared operation. A model-supplied flag cannot replace these
interactions. A transfer acknowledgment establishes receipt and local integrity
only, not completeness of the source account or retention by another provider.

This release does not enumerate a ChatGPT account, recreate native ChatGPT
Projects, perform model inference, or delete source files. It does not fetch
arbitrary source URLs or execute attachments. The original loopback development
adapter remains available for synthetic development; it is not the authenticated
service and must not be exposed remotely.

Supported formats, resource limits, platform restrictions, and known limitations
form part of the release documentation. An operation may fail or reject a source
that is unsupported, changes during processing, or cannot be processed safely.

## 5. Preservation and Structural Limitations

Preservation covers the supported material actually supplied. A source export may
omit images, attachments, message branches, project memberships, timestamps,
permissions, native identifiers, or other relationships. A copied file can retain
its original bytes while still depending on unavailable external resources.

The reference application preserves supported directory relationships and file
contents; it does not interpret every source application's data model. A portable
restore is a recovery of files into a separate directory. It is not a promise to
reconstruct the original application's accounts, workspace layout, permissions,
automations, interface, or consolidated knowledge structures.

The experimental parser reports unsupported or unresolved relationships and keeps
derived views separate from originals. Its current compatibility has been tested
with synthetic material, not a representative real account export. A missing
project identifier cannot, by itself, establish that a conversation is standalone.
A derived view must not be represented as a missing original.

A defect, incomplete export, interrupted operation, storage failure, incompatible
format, or action by another application can result in incomplete recovery,
corrupted output, or loss of useful organization. Review the operation report and
the recovered content before relying on the result.

## 6. Backups and Verification

Maintain an independent copy of important original material and test recovery
into a separate destination. Keep that copy until you have established that the
archive meets your needs. A successful command alone is not a reason to remove
the original or its independent backup.

Local verification compares the archive with its manifest. It does not prove that
an entire external account was captured, that the source export was complete,
or that the original source was authentic. A matching manifest and payload can
both have been changed by an attacker. The current release does not provide a
digital signature or remote integrity verification.

The service's upload and download checks concern bytes held in its configured
environment. Successful delivery does not prove that another device has retained
a durable copy. Verify a downloaded recovery package before relying on it.
